DrWalls

Wi-Fi CSI Motion Detection
Turn your ESP32 into a Wi-Fi motion sensor.
No camera. No PIR sensor. No additional sensing hardware.
1. Connect your ESP32 to your computer using USB.
2. Click INSTALL and select your ESP32 serial port.
3. After installation, connect to DrWalls-Setup.
4. Enter your Wi-Fi details.
5. Open drwalls.local.
